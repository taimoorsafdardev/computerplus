# 🔄 **Return Policy — ComputerPlus Sahiwal**

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains **eligibility, process, and refund options**.

## 📝 **1. Eligibility for Return**

- Products can be returned within **3 days** of purchase  
- Item must be **unused**, in **original packaging**, with all accessories

## ❌ **2. Non-Returnable Items**

- Software, digital keys, or activated devices  
- Opened accessories  
- Items **damaged by the customer**

## 🔄 **3. Return Process**

- Submit a **return request** via WhatsApp or email  
- Bring or **ship the item** to our store  
- **Inspection** will be done before approval

## 💵 **4. Refund Method**

- Refunds are provided as **store credit** or **replacement**  
- **Cash refunds** only in special cases

## 🔁 **5. Exchange**

- Allowed if the product is **defective** or **incorrect**

📌 *By initiating a return, you agree to follow this Return Policy.*
