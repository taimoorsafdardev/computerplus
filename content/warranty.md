# 🛡️ **Warranty Policy — ComputerPlus Sahiwal**

We stand by the quality of our products. This policy explains **coverage, exclusions, and claim procedures**.

## 📦 **1. Coverage**

- All products purchased from **ComputerPlus Sahiwal** come with a **manufacturer or store warranty** (where applicable)
- Warranty duration **varies by product category and brand**

## ✅ **2. What Warranty Covers**

- **Hardware defects**
- **Manufacturing faults**
- Issues occurring under **normal usage conditions**

## ❌ **3. What Warranty Does Not Cover**

- Physical damage, burns, water damage, or misuse
- Unauthorized **repairs or modifications**
- **Software-related issues**, unless specified

## 📝 **4. Claim Process**

- Provide **original invoice/receipt**
- Bring the product to our store or **contact support**
- The product will be **inspected before approval**

## ⏱️ **5. Repair/Replacement Time**

- Depends on **brand and product type**
- **ComputerPlus Sahiwal** is not responsible for delays from manufacturers

## ⚖️ **6. Liability**

- We do not offer **refunds** for warranty cases; only **repair or replacement** if applicable
