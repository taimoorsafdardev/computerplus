# Shipping Policy -- ComputerPlus Sahiwal

## 1. Delivery Coverage

-   We deliver across Pakistan through trusted courier services.

## 2. Delivery Time

-   Standard delivery: **2--5 working days**.
-   Sahiwal city delivery: **Same or next day**.

## 3. Shipping Charges

-   Calculated based on courier rates.
-   Free delivery may be offered during promotions.

## 4. Order Processing Time

-   Orders are processed within **24 hours** after confirmation.

## 5. Damage During Shipping

-   If you receive a damaged parcel, report immediately with photos.
